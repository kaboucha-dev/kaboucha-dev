## Hi, I'm Kaboucha 👋

I build **video automation tools and apps** — from downloading and cleaning clips to rendering ready-to-post vertical videos — with Python, FFmpeg, fast web UIs and Flutter.

- 🎬 **Tahiro** — a short-video studio: on-screen text removal (OpenCV inpainting), a single-pass FFmpeg render at 1080×1920 / 30 fps, AI voiceover with word-timed subtitles, and a background job queue (FastAPI + Redis). *(private)*
- 📱 **NEMATEC** — a Flutter + Firebase marketplace app connecting buyers, suppliers, transporters and creators, with chat, offers and maps. *(private)*
- 🔐 I care about safe code: SSRF protection, rate limiting, encrypted API-key storage.
